About Me

I'm a graphic designer who treats every brief like a small identity problem — figuring out what a brand, an event, or a shirt needs to say before I open the software.

Based in Bukidnon, I work across apparel, print, and digital media for student organizations, local brands, and independent clients. My process starts on paper — sketching layout and hierarchy before touching a screen — because the best designs usually solve themselves in pencil first.

Outside client work, I run a small archive of self-initiated poster and typography studies, which is where most of my experiments end up before they make it into paid work.
