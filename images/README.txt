NOTE: a few filenames in your current index.html have doubled-up
extensions (e.g. "poster-01.jpg.png", "poster-03.jpg.jpg"). Rename
those actual image files to match exactly, or edit the src="" in
index.html to drop the extra extension — otherwise those tiles
will show broken image icons instead of your work.

Tip: photos close to a 4:5 ratio (portrait) fill the tiles best.
Wide tiles (apparel-01, poster-02, branding-03, photo-02) look
best around 8:5 (landscape).
